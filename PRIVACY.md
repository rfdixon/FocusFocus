# Privacy Policy for FocusFocus

**Last Updated:** August 2026

FocusFocus ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
FocusFocus does **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, usage analytics, or telemetry. 

### 2. Local-Only Processing
All calculations and window positioning performed by FocusFocus operate exclusively in local system memory on your Mac. The application does not communicate with external servers or cloud services.

### 3. Accessibility Permissions
FocusFocus requests macOS **Accessibility permissions** (`kAX...` APIs) solely to:
* Detect when windows are moved, resized, miniaturized, or brought to the foreground.
* Position non-interactive dimming overlay windows behind your active window.

FocusFocus does **not** record, capture, log, or inspect the contents of your windows, screen pixels, keystrokes, or personal files.

### 4. Settings Storage
User preferences (such as dimming darkness, depth multiplier, and tint color) are stored locally on your device via standard macOS `UserDefaults`. No settings or configuration files leave your machine.

### 5. Third-Party Services
FocusFocus contains no third-party tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or remote analytics frameworks.
